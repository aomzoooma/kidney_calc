# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

An older Thai caregiver uses a phone while preparing meals for a family member with kidney disease. The caregiver has clinician-provided goals and a kitchen scale.

## Product Purpose

Help the caregiver plan familiar Thai meals and estimate nutrient amounts using recorded ingredients, seasonings and patient-specific goals.

## Operating Context

The first host is an always-on Windows 11 PC. The caregiver uses a phone at home and remotely. The primary seasoning workflow starts by photographing a product label, reviewing and correcting OCR suggestions, saving the product, then reusing it in recipes.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Use Thai language, a mobile-first PWA and locally hosted Typhoon OCR. Missing nutrients remain unknown, not zero. Clinician goals are entered by the user; the app does not prescribe targets. Save only after user review. Ingredient source and serving basis must be traceable. Patient data must remain outside the public repository. Clinical, data-rights, authentication and backup gates remain open.

## Product Principles

- Put the caregiver's next cooking task first.
- Make review and correction of OCR suggestions obvious.
- Preserve missing values and source information honestly.
- Reuse saved ingredients and recipes without rewriting historical meals.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use legible Thai, large touch targets, clear labels and high contrast for an older caregiver using a phone.
